Revenue Size & Growth

Scale and trajectory signal market opportunity and set the floor for valuation multiples.

👤

Owner Dependency

Recurring Revenue

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this just a pitch to sell me something?+
No. The scorecard is genuinely designed to give you useful information about your business. You'll receive your valuation tier and score immediately — no strings attached. If your results suggest a deeper conversation would be valuable, we'll offer a complimentary strategy call. There's no obligation to do anything beyond reviewing your results.
How accurate is the valuation range?+
The scorecard produces an illustrative estimate based on the key value drivers that buyers and PE firms use to evaluate businesses. It's not a formal appraisal — but it gives you a directionally accurate picture of your valuation tier and the factors most likely to move your number. A formal valuation requires a full financial review; this is your starting point. All figures are illustrative only and do not constitute a formal appraisal, legal advice, or financial advice.
Is my information confidential?+
Yes. The information you provide is used solely to generate your scorecard results and is not shared with third parties. We take your privacy seriously and only use your contact details to send your results and, if applicable, to schedule a follow-up conversation if you request one.
I'm not planning to sell anytime soon — is this still relevant for me?+
Absolutely. In fact, the owners who benefit most from this are the ones who aren't selling yet. Knowing your valuation now gives you years to close the gaps that reduce your multiple — gaps that take time to fix. If you wait until you're ready to sell to find out your business is worth less than you thought, you've lost your window to change it.
What's the difference between a valuation and an exit plan?+
A valuation tells you what your business is worth today. An exit plan tells you how to turn that value into cash or income for you — and maps the specific path (sale, succession, transition) to get there on your timeline. You need both. The scorecard gives you the valuation picture; the strategy call helps you build the plan.
What happens after I submit the scorecard?+
You'll be redirected immediately to your results page, which shows your valuation tier, exit readiness score, and the specific value drivers that have the biggest impact on your business. From there, you can book a complimentary Business Worth Strategy Call to go deeper — or simply take your results and use them on your own. Either way, the information is yours.
